Miller Johnson is an **internal case and invoice management portal** built for a law firm's eDiscovery team. It helps staff track active legal matters, manage third-party vendor charges, and coordinate monthly invoice approvals.

## My contribution

I designed and built workflows for managing legal matters, tracking vendor expenses, and processing invoice approvals. Working across design and frontend development, I translated the team's operational requirements into a tool that brought case-related financial information and billing workflows into one place.

The project involved understanding how eDiscovery staff, paralegals, and billing attorneys work together, then building interfaces to support their day-to-day tasks.

## Impact

The tool was designed to streamline invoice management and reduce manual coordination across the monthly approval process.
