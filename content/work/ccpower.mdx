CCPower gives fleet teams one platform to manage inspections, repairs, and finances.

## My contribution

I developed the inspection, repair, and billing modules, building core workflows used to manage fleet operations. My work focused on implementing the interfaces and functionality that help teams track inspections, coordinate repairs, and manage billing within one system.

## Technical stack

I worked with Vue 3, TypeScript, Quasar, Pinia, and Vitest to build and maintain the application.
