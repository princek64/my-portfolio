UX design placement at GetSociable, a consumer mobile app for discovering social events and meeting people. Working embedded with the product and engineering team.

## UX audit

Ran a full UX audit of the live app covering onboarding, home feed, discovery, map, itinerary planning, and social features. Cross-referenced the audit against existing design documentation and findings from two rounds of user testing.

## Design system audit

Audited the existing Figma design system for consistency gaps, covering component naming, spacing, colour usage, and interaction states. Produced prioritised findings for stakeholder review.

## Current work

Currently working on translating audit findings into design improvements and updating the design system to close the gaps identified.
