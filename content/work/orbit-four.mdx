**Orbit Four** is a **Domain Name Registrar and Management Portal**. It's a platform designed to allow users to search for, purchase, and manage internet domains, as well as handle their associated DNS and contact records. 
It interfaces with the Extensible Provisioning Protocol (EPP) to register domains directly with registries.
