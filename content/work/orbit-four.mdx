**Orbit Four** is a **Domain Name Registrar and Management platform** that allows users to search for, register, and manage internet domains. It also provides tools for managing DNS settings and domain contact records, connecting to registries through the Extensible Provisioning Protocol (EPP).

## My contribution
I worked on the frontend application, building interfaces and workflows for domain search and management. The platform brought domain registration and ongoing administration into one place, including the configuration of DNS and contact records.

My work involved translating domain management requirements into functional interfaces and integrating them with the platform's underlying services.

## Technical stack

Nuxt, Vue, TypeScript, and the supporting APIs used by the application.
