**Product Design · Next.js · MapLibre · 2026**

A website for Marmalade Trust, made with Scottish Tech Army, that helps people who'd otherwise be alone on Christmas Day find a local event. Visitors search a postcode or town and see what's near them. Hosts list their event through a short form. Marmalade Trust staff check every event before it goes live.

---

## The Problem

Community Christmas events happen all over the UK, but their details are scattered across council pages, church newsletters and local Facebook groups. Someone who is isolated, and doesn't know where to look, can easily miss them.

Many of the people the site is for are older, and many arrive on a phone. So the site has to be easy to read, easy to tap, and work on a weak signal.

---

## My Role

I lead the design and build, and work directly with Marmalade Trust. The project runs through Scottish Tech Army, so the work had to fit the time a small volunteer team could give it.

---

## What changed as it grew

It started as a map of events. It's now three things in one site:

- **For visitors:** search by postcode or town, see a list first (the client's choice) with a map one tap away, and filter by what matters: a meal, step-free access, free, no booking.
- **For hosts:** a five-step form to list an event, and a private link to change or withdraw it later. No accounts and no passwords.
- **For staff:** a portal with a to-do list (new events to approve, possible duplicates, events missing accessibility details), and an overview of what's live.

---

## Design decisions

- **Readable for older eyes.** Facts people act on, like the time, place and cost, are bold and navy. Grey is only for secondary lines, never small. Everything you can tap has a clear edge.
- **Never guess.** If a host hasn't said whether an event is free or step-free, the site shows nothing rather than a wrong badge. Unknown booking reads "Ask the host".
- **A map that works on a weak signal.** The map moved from picture tiles to a vector map. Pins that sit close together join into numbered bubbles, so every event can be reached. If part of the map fails to load, the pins still show.
- **Built for desktop too.** Analytics showed many visitors were on a computer, where a Call button does nothing. Phone numbers can now be copied as well as called.
- **Christmas Day itself.** On the day, the site greets people with "It's Christmas Day" and marks events as "Today" or "On now". The logo puts on a costume through December.
- **When nothing is nearby.** Some towns have no events yet. Instead of a dead end, people can let the charity know where events are needed, try a wider search, or play a short Christmas game while they wait.
