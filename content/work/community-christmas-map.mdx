A website for Marmalade Trust, made with Scottish Tech Army, that helps people who'd otherwise be alone on Christmas Day find a local event.

---

## The problem

Community Christmas events are scattered across council pages, church newsletters and Facebook groups. The people who need them most are often older, on a phone, and on a weak signal.

## My role

I lead the design and build, working directly with Marmalade Trust.

## What it does

- **Visitors** search a postcode or town and get a list, with the map one tap away.
- **Hosts** list an event in five short steps, and change it later through a private link. No accounts.
- **Staff** approve every event before it goes live, from a to-do list in their portal.

## Key decisions

- **Readable for older eyes:** the facts people act on are bold, and everything tappable has a clear edge.
- **Never guess:** if a host hasn't said whether it's free or step-free, the site shows nothing rather than a wrong badge.
- **A map that copes with a weak signal:** nearby pins join into numbered bubbles, and the pins still show if part of the map fails to load.
- **A bit of Christmas:** on the day it greets people with "It's Christmas Day", and when nothing is nearby there's a short game while they wait.
